    Terrain Studio

    Architectural visualization and spatial design. Each project begins with the land, the light, and the people who will inhabit it. This sheet lets you check all three.

    Three principles, three instruments

    • Material first.Materials are chosen for how they age and weather. Place a volume and see what the ground asks of it: slope, cut, fill.
    • Light as form.Light is structure. The shadows on every sheet come from the sun's position at that latitude, date and hour.
    • Human scale.Architecture serves the body before the eye. Draw a walk and read the climb, the minutes, and where a ramp would fail.

    About this sheet

    Terrain is a fictional studio. The five landforms are generated, not surveyed. Sun positions use a standard low-precision solar formula and each site's real coordinates and time zone, and are good to about a degree.
