# HumanLup Design Lab · posts
One card and one caption per piece. Cards are 1080 × 1350 (saved at 2x) in each piece's folder as `card.jpg`.

## 02 · Terracotta

A color library shelved like glaze test tiles. 138 colors, sorted by pigment. I like that the rim of each tile runs thin, the way real glaze does.

Folder: `_revival/002-terracotta/`

## 03 · Tidal

A design language for buildings at the water's edge. The whole page sits on a tide line that keeps moving. I could watch the water rise all day.

Folder: `_revival/003-tidal/`

## 04 · Terrain

A landscape studio page where the sun is actually computed. Drag it across the day and the shadows move over the contours. The ground is made up, the light is not.

Folder: `_revival/004-terrain/`

## 05 · Cinema

A fifty second film drawn in the browser, seven lines long. Press play. Fable 5.2 did really well with the motion on this one.

Folder: `_revival/005-cinema/`

## 06 · Taste

An essay on taste with a slider under it. Drag left and it edits itself down to five percent.

Folder: `_revival/006-taste-report/`

## 07 · Prints

Every color gets a fingerprint drawn from its own hex. Type yours in and it tells you if Tailwind already has it. The ridges are my favorite part.

Folder: `_revival/007-ink-almanac/`

## 08 · Reflecction

A daily read on how I use AI. Every prompt from the day on one sheet, with a short story of what worked. The double c is on purpose.

Folder: `_revival/008-reflecction/`

## 09 · Press L

Press L and the page you are reading becomes the canvas. Drag things around and it writes up what you changed.

Folder: `_revival/009-layout-mode/`

## 10 · Glass

Wanted glass that acts like glass. Drag a piece over the type, move the lamp, and it gives you the CSS that gets closest.

Folder: `_revival/010-glass/`

## 11 · Rare Form

One magazine issue, cut four ways. Slide the imbalance up and the type hangs a little off axis. I wanted to see how far off it could go and still feel right.

Folder: `_revival/011-l-editorial/`

## 12 · Tool Library

A neighborhood tool library. Say when you need it by and it tells you what you can actually get your hands on. Love the lime.

Folder: `_revival/012-tool-library/`

## 13 · Swipe File

Counted every pixel on five sites I like. Turns out the color you remember isn't always the one that's there.

Folder: `_revival/013-swipe-file/`

## 14 · Only Copy

Which repos exist only on this laptop? Red means it's the only copy. Pick what to back up, archive or drop and it writes the plan for you. The folder on the page is made up. The nested boxes came out better than I expected.

Folder: `_revival/014-only-copy/`

## 15 · No Script

How far does a page get with no JavaScript? Pretty far. The headline turns into a code face as you scroll, and the bar at the bottom peels the page back to bare HTML. The scroll feels great.

Folder: `_revival/015-layer-issue/`

## 16 · Field Notes

My notes on the LLM wiki idea, as 71 pages on a ring. Click a name and you can see what links to it. It also checks itself for loose ends.

Folder: `_revival/016-wiki-of-the-wiki/`

## 17 · Set Board

A weekly board for a climbing gym. Walls across, grades up, and anything older than six weeks comes down. I do not set routes, I just like the grid.

Folder: `_revival/017-set-board/`

## 18 · One Line

A week of my scheduled jobs, drawn one line per run. Scroll and the week goes by. The quiet stretch in the middle is when I am working.

Folder: `_revival/018-one-line-a-run/`

## 19 · Seven Grays

Seven grays, one black and a dither made with nothing but CSS. Move the pointer and the sun follows. Love how the mountains turned out.

Folder: `_revival/019-seven-grays/`

## 20 · Your Line

Draw one line anywhere and the page lays itself out around it. Seven squares follow your hand. One of my favorites from this batch.

Folder: `_revival/020-your-line-first/`

## 21 · Let’s Talk

A contact page that is just a conversation. Change the green words and it writes the email for you. Thought it was a good idea for a studio of one.

Folder: `_revival/021-lets-talk/`

## 23 · Call Sheet

A prompt laid out like a film call sheet. Fill the lines, pull tabs from the margin, send what comes out the side. You are not writing code, you are directing.

Folder: `_revival/023-call-sheet/`

## 24 · Grow

What if a brand could grow? Love how the flowers turned out, thought it was a good idea for a creative studio. Fable 5.2 did really well with the animation on this.

Folder: `_revival/024-you-need-both/`

## 25 · Eight

A style guide on 8 px squared paper. Pull a handle and the button grows one square at a time. The page counts its own boxes to check they all sit on a line.

Folder: `_revival/025-eight-at-a-time/`

## 26 · Any Color

A wall of dots where every dot is a different color at the same lightness. Type on it and the words show up one step darker. Fable 5.2 nailed the color math.

Folder: `_revival/026-any-color/`

## 27 · One Card

One social card that holds at any shape. Drag the corner and the words find their own place. Love how the words settle.

Folder: `_revival/027-one-card/`

## 28 · Two Ls

Two Ls make a W. Tap and you take one, tap again and the pair tips over into a W. Hold down and the page fills up. Love the springs on this one.

Folder: `_revival/028-take-the-l/`

## 29 · O’clock

A clock you count. The bumps are the hour and the dot is the minute. At six the shape takes over the page and it's night. Fable 5.2 got the edge just right.

Folder: `_revival/029-oclock/`

## 30 · Nosy

A page full of little guys that watch your cursor. Moving across them sends a wave, and if you sit still long enough they get bored and nod off. The little lag is what makes it.

Folder: `_revival/030-nosy/`

## 31 · Fold

A sheet of paper you can fold. Grab it anywhere and drag. Unfold it and the creases are still there. Still surprised the geometry works.

Folder: `_revival/031-fold/`
