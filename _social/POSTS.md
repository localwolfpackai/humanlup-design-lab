## Captions

**Lab 02, Terracotta.** 138 colors, each one fired as a little test tile. I like that the rim runs thin like real glaze. Fable 5.2 got the shading right on the first pass.

**Lab 03, Tidal.** A design language for buildings at the water's edge. The whole page sits on a tide line that keeps moving.

**Lab 04, Terrain.** A made-up architecture studio where the shadows are real. Pick a site, drag the sun, and it lands where it would at that spot on that day.

**Lab 05, Cinema.** Short films made from nothing but words and light. Type a few lines, hit play, and it cuts itself.

**Lab 06, Taste.** An essay on taste with a slider under it. Drag left and it edits itself down to five percent.

**Lab 07, Prints.** Every color gets a fingerprint drawn from its own hex. Type yours in and it tells you if Tailwind already has it. The ridges are my favorite part.

**Lab 08, Reflecction.** A day of prompts on the left, what it says about me on the right. Love how the lines run back to the prompts they came from.

**Lab 09, Press L.** Press L and the page you are reading becomes the canvas. Drag things around and it writes up what you changed.

**Lab 10, Glass.** Wanted glass that acts like glass. Drag a piece over the type, move the lamp, and it gives you the CSS that gets closest.

**Lab 11, Rare Form.** One magazine issue set four ways with nothing but type. There's a dial for how off-balance it gets. It starts at 25.

**Lab 12, Tool Library.** A neighborhood tool library with one question up top: can I have it by Saturday? Thought the date should be the biggest thing on the page.

**Lab 13, Swipe File.** Counted every pixel on five sites I like. Turns out the color you remember isn't always the one that's there.

**Lab 16, Field Notes.** My notes on the LLM wiki idea, as 71 pages on a ring. Click a name and you can see what links to it.

**Lab 17, Set Plan.** A week of route setting for a climbing gym on one board. Walls across, grades up, and anything older than six weeks comes down.

**Lab 18, One Line.** How I work, drawn one line for each time a job runs, for one week.

**Lab 19, Seven Grays.** Seven grays, one black and a dither made with nothing but CSS. Move the pointer and the sun follows. Love how the mountains turned out.

**Lab 20, Your Line.** Draw one line and the headline moves out of its way. Still one of my favorites. Fable 5.2 did really well with how the squares follow your hand.

**Lab 21, Let's Talk.** A contact page with no form. You change three sentences and it writes the email for you.

**Lab 23, Call Sheet.** A call sheet for a prompt. Fill five lines and it comes out the side.

**Lab 24, Grow.** Love how the flowers turned out, thought it was a good idea for a creative studio. Fable 5.2 did really well with the animation on this.

**Lab 25, Eight.** A style guide where every box sits on an 8 px line, and the page checks itself. Pull a handle and the button grows one square at a time.

**Lab 26, Any Color.** A wall of dots where every dot is a different color at the same lightness. Type on it and the words show up one step darker. Fable 5.2 nailed the color math.

## 27 · One Card

One social card that holds at any shape. Drag the corner and the words find their own place. Love how the words settle.

Folder: `_revival/027-one-card/`

## 28 · Two Ls

Two Ls make a W. Tap and you take one, tap again and the pair tips over into a W. Hold down and the page fills up. Love the springs on this one.

Folder: `_revival/028-take-the-l/`

## 14 · Only Copy

Which repos exist only on this laptop? Red means it's the only copy. Pick what to back up, archive or drop and it writes the plan for you. The folder on the page is made up. The nested boxes came out better than I expected.

Folder: `_revival/014-only-copy/`

## 15 · No Script

How far does a page get with no JavaScript? Pretty far. The headline turns into a code face as you scroll, and the bar at the bottom peels the page back to bare HTML. The scroll feels great.

Folder: `_revival/015-layer-issue/`

## 29 · O’clock

A clock you count. The bumps are the hour and the dot is the minute. At six the shape takes over the page and it's night. Fable 5.2 got the edge just right.

Folder: `_revival/029-oclock/`

## 30 · Nosy

A page full of little guys that watch your cursor. Moving across them sends a wave, and if you sit still long enough they get bored and nod off. The little lag is what makes it.

Folder: `_revival/030-nosy/`

## 31 · Fold

A sheet of paper you can fold. Grab it anywhere and drag. Unfold it and the creases are still there. Still surprised the geometry works.

Folder: `_revival/031-fold/`
