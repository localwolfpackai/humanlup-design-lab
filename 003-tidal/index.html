Tidal

A design language for buildings that stand at the water's edge. It has two states, dry and wet, and the tide decides which one you are looking at.

The model tide stands at 0.00 m and is rising. High water soon. Scroll, and everything on the page passes through the surface.

02 / Material

Color as material

Five materials from the shore. Each has a dry value and a wet value, because stone, timber and sand all darken when water reaches them. Wet is the same hue at 82 percent of the lightness, measured in OKLab.
